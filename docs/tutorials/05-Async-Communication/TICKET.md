# Teams Communication & Collaboration — Getting Started

## What This Is About

In a remote work environment, every message you send and every interaction you have on Teams contributes to your professional reputation. This task is about learning how to communicate on Teams the right way — these habits will carry you through the entire mentorship program and serve you well throughout your career.

## How-to Guide

Take about 5 minutes to read through this document carefully:

📄 **[Teams Communication & Collaboration Quick Guide](https://github.com/easyscale-academy/learn_easyscale_academy_essentials-project/tree/05-Async-Communication/)**

As you read, pay close attention to these key ideas:

- **Async communication** — You're not expected to be online around the clock, but you should check in and respond in a reasonable timeframe
- **Quote Reply** — An essential habit for keeping threaded conversations organized, especially when multiple topics are in play
- **Screen recording** — The clearest way to walk someone through an issue, and great practice for technical interviews
- **Professional communication principles** — Always follow up, always follow through, always close the loop
