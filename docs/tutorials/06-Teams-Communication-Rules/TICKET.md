# Set Up Teams as Your Official Communication Channel

## Objective

Establish Microsoft Teams as your primary communication tool for all project-related discussions. By the end of this task, you'll have Teams installed and running on both your computer and phone, and you'll understand the communication boundaries for this program.

Read the tutorial: [Teams Communication Rules](https://github.com/easyscale-academy/learn_easyscale_academy_essentials-project/tree/06-Teams-Communication-Rules)

## Actionable Items

1. Read the full tutorial document to understand why we use Teams instead of personal messaging apps
2. Sign in to Teams on your computer at [teams.microsoft.com](https://teams.microsoft.com) using your company email
3. Install the Microsoft Teams app on your phone and sign in with the same company email
4. Join the group chat your mentor created for you
5. Send a confirmation message in the group chat to verify everything works on both devices

**Estimated time:** 10-15 minutes

## Checklist

- [ ] **Read the tutorial** — Understand the rule: all project communication goes through Teams, personal channels are for emergencies only
- [ ] **Teams on computer** — Signed in at teams.microsoft.com with your company email
- [ ] **Teams on phone** — Installed the Microsoft Teams app and signed in successfully
- [ ] **Joined the group chat** — Can open the mentor's group chat on both computer and phone
- [ ] **Sent a confirmation message** — Posted a hello message in the group chat to verify everything works

---

## Grading Rubric

- **Teams access on computer:** Student can open teams.microsoft.com and is signed in with their company email
- **Teams access on phone:** Student has the Microsoft Teams mobile app installed and can open it while signed in
- **Group chat participation:** Student has sent at least one message in the mentor's group chat, confirming both devices work
- **Understanding of the rule:** Student can explain in their own words what communication belongs on Teams vs. personal channels
- **Multi-device verification:** Student confirms they can see the same group chat and messages on both computer and phone
