# Practice Task: Plan and Set Up Your Email Accounts

## Objective

Register your two primary email accounts — Gmail and Outlook — to build a solid digital identity foundation for studying and launching your career in the United States.

## How-to Guide

Read the tutorial *[Essential Email Account Planning Guide for International Students in the U.S.](https://github.com/easyscale-academy/learn_easyscale_academy_essentials-project/tree/03-Email-Account-Planning/)* and follow the instructions to register your accounts and configure security settings.

Pay special attention to:

- **Naming format** — use a professional `firstnamelastname+number` pattern
- **Cross-linking recovery emails** — a critical security step
- **Enabling Two-Factor Authentication**

**Estimated time: 20–30 minutes**

## Checklist

- [ ] **Register a Gmail account** — use a professional naming format, e.g. `john.doe@gmail.com`
- [ ] **Register an Outlook account** — follow the same naming convention
- [ ] **Link recovery emails both ways** — set Gmail as Outlook's recovery email and vice versa
- [ ] **Turn on Two-Factor Authentication** — enable it on both accounts
